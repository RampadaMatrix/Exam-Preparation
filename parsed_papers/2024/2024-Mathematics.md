**1. Choose the correct alternative for each question: [1 × 6 = 6]**

**1.1** In a business, the ratio of capital of three persons is $3 : 8 : 5$ and the share of profit of the first person is ₹60 less than that of the third person. The total profit in the business was:  
(a) ₹240  
(b) ₹360  
(c) ₹480  
(d) ₹540  

**1.2** If the roots of the quadratic equation $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$ ($a \neq 0$) are real and unequal, then the value of $b^2 - 4ac$ will be:  
(a) $> 0$  
(b) $= 0$  
(c) $< 0$  
(d) None of these  

**1.3** The lengths of two chords $AB$ and $CD$ of a circle with center $O$ are equal. If $\angle AOB = 60^\circ$, then the measure of $\angle COD$ is:  
(a) $30^\circ$  
(b) $60^\circ$  
(c) $120^\circ$  
(d) $90^\circ$  

**1.4** If the radius of the base of a right circular cone is kept constant and its height is doubled, the volume of the cone increases by:  
(a) $100\%$  
(b) $200\%$  
(c) $300\%$  
(d) $50\%$  

**1.5** If $\sin \theta - \cos \theta = 0$ ($0^\circ \leq \theta \leq 90^\circ$) and $\sec \theta + \csc \theta = x$, then the value of $x$ is:  
(a) $1$  
(b) $2$  
(c) $\sqrt{2}$  
(d) $2\sqrt{2}$  

**1.6** The mode of the numbers $3, 5, 8, 3, 5, 7, 5, 8, 3, x$ is $3$. The value of $x$ must be:  
(a) $5$  
(b) $8$  
(c) $3$  
(d) $7$
