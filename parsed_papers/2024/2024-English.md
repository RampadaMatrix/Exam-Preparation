## Section ‘A’: Reading Comprehension (Seen & Unseen)

### 1. Seen Comprehension: Prose & Poetry [20 Marks]

**Read the following excerpt carefully and answer the questions that follow:**

> *The cat does not simply live on milk and mice. Most people think that the cat is an unintelligent animal, fond of ease and caring little for anything but mice and milk. But a cat has really more character than most human beings, and gets a great deal more satisfaction out of life.*  
> *He or she is an athlete, an acrobat, and a grim fighter. All day long the cat loafs about the house, takes things easy, and allows himself to be pestered by the attentions of the people in the house. To pass the time away he sometimes watches a mouse-hole for an hour or two — just to keep himself from dying of boredom.*

**A. Write the correct alternative in the given box: [1 × 5 = 5]**

1. Most people think that the cat is:  
   (a) an athlete  
   (b) an unintelligent animal  
   (c) a grim fighter  
   (d) an acrobat  
2. The cat watches a mouse-hole:  
   (a) out of hunger  
   (b) to kill boredom  
   (c) to exercise  
   (d) to please the master  
3. In reality, a cat has:  
   (a) less intelligence than a dog  
   (b) more character than most humans  
   (c) no sense of fun  
   (d) fear of strangers  
4. All day long the cat:  
   (a) hunts mice  
   (b) sleeps on the terrace  
   (c) loafs about the house  
   (d) plays with balls  
5. The cat is described as an:  
   (a) athlete, an acrobat, and a fighter  
   (b) idle and selfish beast  
   (c) affectionate pet  
   (d) obedient servant  

**B. Complete the following sentences with information from the text: [1 × 3 = 3]**
1. The common belief of most people about cats is that ________.
2. In reality, a cat gets far more ________.
3. The cat allows himself to be pestered by ________.

**C. Answer the following questions: [2 × 2 = 4]**
1. Why does the narrator describe the cat as an "athlete and an acrobat"?
2. What does the cat do to keep himself from dying of boredom?

### Poetry Section (Seen)

**Read the following stanza and answer the questions:**

> *Once I crept in an oakwood — I was looking for a stag.*  
> *I met an old woman there — all knobbly stick and rag.*  
> *She said: ‘I have your secret here inside my little bag.’*  
> *Then she began to cackle and I began to quake.*  
> *She opened up her little bag and I came twice awake —*  
> *Surrounded by a staring tribe and me tied to a stake.*

**A. Choose the correct alternative: [1 × 4 = 4]**
1. The poet was looking for a: (a) doe (b) stag (c) rabbit (d) horse
2. The old woman's stick was: (a) straight (b) knobbly (c) polished (d) iron
3. When the woman began to cackle, the poet began to: (a) laugh (b) dance (c) quake (d) run
4. The poet found himself tied to a: (a) tree (b) stake (c) pillar (d) rock

**B. Answer the following questions: [2 × 2 = 4]**
1. Who did the poet meet when he crept into the oakwood?
2. What happened when the old woman opened her little bag?

### 2. Unseen Comprehension [20 Marks]

**Read the passage on India’s historic space milestone and answer the questions:**

> *On August 23, 2023, India scripted historic space glory when the Indian Space Research Organisation’s (ISRO) Chandrayaan-3 spacecraft successfully soft-landed its Vikram lander module on the unexplored South Pole region of the Moon. With this breathtaking technological feat, India became the first country in the world to reach the lunar south pole, and only the fourth nation after the USA, the former Soviet Union, and China to achieve a successful lunar landing.*  
> *The mission aimed to demonstrate safe and soft touchdown on lunar terrain, deploy the Pragyan rover to conduct in-situ chemical and geological analyses of the lunar soil, and search for water ice deposits. Scientists around the world applauded the frugal engineering and sheer dedication of ISRO scientists.*

**A. Tick the correct alternative: [1 × 6 = 6]**
1. The Vikram lander touched down on the Moon on:  
   (a) August 15, 2023 (b) August 23, 2023 (c) September 2, 2023 (d) July 14, 2023
2. India is the first country to land on the Moon's:  
   (a) North Pole (b) Equator (c) South Pole (d) Far side
3. The rover deployed from the Vikram lander was named:  
   (a) Pragyan (b) Aditya (c) Mangalyaan (d) Gaganyaan
4. How many countries had landed spacecraft on the Moon before India?  
   (a) One (b) Two (c) Three (d) Four
5. One of the prime scientific goals of the mission was to:  
   (a) build a lunar hotel (b) detect water ice deposits (c) mine gold (d) bring back rocks
6. The global scientific community praised ISRO for its:  
   (a) lavish budget (b) frugal engineering (c) secrecy (d) political speeches

**B. State whether the following statements are True or False. Provide Supporting Statements (S.S.): [2 × 3 = 6]**
1. India was the second country to reach the lunar south pole. [T/F]  
   *S.S.:* __________________________________________________
2. The Pragyan rover was designed to study the composition of lunar soil. [T/F]  
   *S.S.:* __________________________________________________
3. Chandrayaan-3 proved the capabilities of soft landing on the lunar surface. [T/F]  
   *S.S.:* __________________________________________________

**C. Answer the following questions: [2 × 4 = 8]**
1. What was the historic achievement of Chandrayaan-3 on August 23, 2023?
2. Which nations achieved lunar landings prior to India?
3. What were the main scientific objectives of the Pragyan rover?
4. Why is the engineering of ISRO admired worldwide?
